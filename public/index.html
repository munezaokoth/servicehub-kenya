About ServiceHub Kenya

Transforming ideas into reality, one service at a time across all 47 counties

Our Story & Mission

ServiceHub Kenya was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to create a seamless bridge between skilled service providers and those in need of reliable services across all 47 counties of Kenya. We believe that every idea deserves to become reality, and every need should be met with professionalism and excellence.

In a country with immense talent and diverse service needs, we saw an opportunity to transform how Kenyans connect with service professionals. From urban centers like Nairobi and Mombasa to rural communities, ServiceHub brings convenience, trust, and quality to your doorstep.

Our Mission

To revolutionize service delivery in Kenya by connecting verified professionals with clients through a trusted, efficient, and transparent platform across all 47 counties.

Our Vision

To become Kenya's leading service marketplace, empowering communities and driving economic growth through accessible professional services nationwide.

Our Values

Trust, Excellence, Innovation, Community, and Accessibility guide every aspect of our platform and operations across Kenya.

How ServiceHub Works

Simple steps to get the service you need anywhere in Kenya

100% free for clients and service providers. No fees, no commissions.

1

Post a Job — Free

Describe what you need, your location and budget. Posting is free.

2

Get Free Quotes

Service providers near you send their quotes at no cost to them or you.

3

Compare & Choose

Open each quote, chat with providers, then accept the one that suits you.

4

Provider Accepts

The provider confirms the job and sets a start date. A job chat opens for both of you.

5

Get It Done

The provider does the work while you keep in touch through the job chat.

6

Pay the Provider Directly

Pay the agreed amount straight to the provider by cash, M-Pesa or bank. ServiceHub takes no commission and never handles your money.

7

Mark as Completed

Confirm the job is done. Completed jobs stay private in your dashboard.
